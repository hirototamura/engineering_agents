What happens inside the control loop

h = Jω
x = [tilt_x, tilt_y, rate_x, rate_y, momentum_x, momentum_y]
u = −Kx · plus a separate yaw-rate damper
JLQR = Σ (xᵀQx + uᵀRu)

Q changes the penalty on tilt, angular rate, and momentum; R penalizes control effort. The LQR objective J differs from the post-experiment search score S. The actuator limits commands using finite torque and speed capacity.

What the score measures

Tilt magnitude uses the root mean square (RMS): square the values, take their mean, and take the square root. A gate is an individual pass/fail condition; S is the exploration score used to rank designs.

S = passing cases / 12
− 0.05 × mean(final-second tilt RMS in degrees)
− 0.2 × mean(torque-threshold exceedance fraction)

A case passes only when every gate passes. S is a dimensionless exploration proxy, not a probability of physical success. It does not measure electricity use or temperature.
