Actor survival — 50 points top outcome

Score the fraction of actors still alive at simulation end, scaled directly to points.

50 × crew_remaining ÷ crew_initial
  • Requires plant_sim + survival enabled. Crew count and actor count are synchronized as the same people.
  • Outputs: crew_initial, crew_remaining, crew_lost, crew_lost_by_cause.
  • Separate physical-floor loss from band-dwell rule loss:
    • Physical floor: inventory cannot physically cover the next step's demand
    • Band-dwell rule: exposure time in a configured danger band exceeds tolerance

A. Time to Crew Loss (TCL) — 10 points

Evaluate how long until the first crew loss if the current failure state continues.

TCL = time of first crew_lost event
score = 10 × min(TCL ÷ T_ref, 1)
  • Fix T_ref (full-credit horizon) and failure-continuation assumptions before scoring.
  • Canonical source: simulation_time_s at the event step. Per-cause TCL is retained.
  • No loss through T_ref: 10 points. Run ends before T_ref with no loss: right-censored (insufficient observation time, not failure) — total score incomplete.

Never extrapolate beyond the run end. Continue the simulation with the same failures if a longer horizon is needed.

B. Survival environment actors were placed in — 10 points

Evaluate how deeply and for how long actors were exposed to hazardous conditions across the run.

  • CO₂: excess above limit × duration
  • O₂: deficit below limit × duration
  • Product water: deficit below limit × duration
  • Steps spent in safe / warning / critical
  • Longest consecutive critical streak

Score the trajectory, not snapshots — distinguish brief minor excursions from severe prolonged exposure.

C. Resource margin and recovery the design provided — 10 points

Evaluate whether the design/system survived the worst phase and recovered resource state by run end.

  • CO₂: initial, at-event, peak, terminal, margin to safe upper bound
  • O₂: initial, at-event, minimum, terminal, margin to safe lower bound
  • Product water: initial, at-event, minimum, terminal, margin to safe lower bound
  • Terminal safe / warning / critical
  • Terminal not-recovered flag

Events are fault/anomaly steps; use pre-operation telemetry at that step. Also report delta from initial values.

Axes added only when actor operations are enabled — 20 points total

D. Actor operational decisions — 10 points actor factor

Evaluate when, what, and how much was requested before commands reach devices, against observed state.

  • Latency from dangerous state to appropriate first action
  • Appropriateness of command kind and requested amount for the state
  • Invalid commands after observed failure or shortage; unnecessary duplicate commands

E. Design / device physical response — 10 points design factor

After a physically valid request, evaluate how much the device converted the request into actual processing.

  • Success rate of valid commands; requested vs processed amount
  • ΔCO₂, ΔO₂, Δwater before/after operation; capacity saturation
  • Side effects; residual capability and alternate paths under failure

Out of scope and operational notes

  • Do not treat LLM self-evaluation, message volume, or proposal count as success metrics.
  • Do not describe virtual verification pass as verified on real hardware or in the physical world.
  • Record backend, initial inventories, crew size, failure conditions, steps, and thresholds in evaluation results.
